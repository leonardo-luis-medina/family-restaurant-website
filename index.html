Our Story

A Small Restaurant
with a Big Heart

Verde is a small local restaurant in Poblacion 1, Tanauan, Batangas, dedicated to serving well-loved Filipino dishes made with quality ingredients and genuine care.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries the warmth of home — familiar flavors cooked the right way, for the people we love to feed.
